Passu Glacier and Hussaini Bridge

PKR 32,000/person
Duration 2 Days
Difficulty Easy
Group Size 2-15 People
Max Altitude 2,800 m
About this trip

If you only have a couple of days, this is the stretch of the Karakoram Highway to spend them on. Upper Hunza between Gulmit and Passu has the Cathedral Ridge - the Passu Cones - on one side and the Batura and Passu glaciers on the other.

The Hussaini bridge deserves its reputation: widely spaced planks, a long span, and a lot of wind. Crossing is entirely optional and nobody will judge you.

Trip highlights
  • Passu Cones from the classic viewpoint
  • Walk to the Passu Glacier snout
  • Hussaini suspension bridge crossing
  • Borith Lake and its migrating birds
  • Gulmit village and the old polo ground
What's included
  • One night in a Passu or Gulmit guesthouse
  • Transport from Karimabad or Gilgit
  • Local guide
  • Breakfast and dinner
Not included
  • Lunches
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal expenses
  • Tips
Itinerary
Day 1 – To Passu
Drive up from Karimabad, stopping at Attabad Lake and Gulmit. Afternoon walk to Borith Lake. Overnight Passu.
Day 2 – Glacier and bridge
Morning walk to the Passu Glacier viewpoint, then the Hussaini bridge before driving back down the valley.
