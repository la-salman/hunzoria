Hunza Valley Explorer

PKR 145,000/person
Duration 7 Days
Difficulty Easy
Group Size 4-12 People
Max Altitude 2,400 m
About this trip

Hunza is the valley most people picture when they imagine northern Pakistan: terraced apricot orchards stacked against 7,000-metre peaks, and a pace of life that rewards staying still. This trip is built around that pace. We base ourselves in Karimabad for several nights rather than moving hotels every day, and take day trips out from there.

You will walk the old town lanes below Baltit Fort, watch sunrise on Rakaposhi from Duikar, take a boat across Attabad Lake, and cross the Hussaini bridge if the wind allows. It suits first-time visitors, families, and anyone who wants the Karakoram without a serious trek.

Trip highlights
  • Baltit and Altit forts, both restored by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture
  • Sunrise over Rakaposhi and Golden Peak from Duikar viewpoint
  • Boat crossing on Attabad Lake
  • Passu Cones, Passu Glacier viewpoint and the Hussaini suspension bridge
  • Apricot and cherry orchards in season, and Hunza walnut cake
What's included
  • All accommodation (guesthouses and small hotels)
  • Private vehicle and driver for the full trip
  • English-speaking local guide from Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Daily breakfast and dinner
  • All entry fees and permits
Not included
  • Flights or transport to Gilgit / Islamabad
  • Lunches and personal expenses
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips for guide and driver
Itinerary
Day 1 – Arrive Gilgit
Fly or drive into Gilgit, meet your guide and driver, and settle in. Short walk through the bazaar in the evening to shake off the journey.
Day 2 – Gilgit to Karimabad
Drive up the Karakoram Highway to Karimabad, stopping at the Rakaposhi viewpoint in Ghulmet for lunch. Afternoon free to wander the old town.
Day 3 – Baltit and Altit forts
A full day on foot in Karimabad and Altit - both forts, the old settlement lanes, and the royal garden. Easy walking, lots of stopping.
Day 4 – Duikar sunrise and Attabad Lake
Early start for sunrise at Duikar, then down to Attabad Lake for a boat crossing and a long lunch by the water.
Day 5 – Upper Hunza - Passu and Hussaini
Drive north to Gulmit and Passu. Walk to the Passu Glacier viewpoint and cross the Hussaini suspension bridge if conditions allow.
Day 6 – Karimabad at your own pace
A deliberately unscheduled day. Walk the Ultar Nala trail, visit a local workshop, or do very little.
Day 7 – Return to Gilgit
Drive back down the KKH to Gilgit for onward flights or the road to Islamabad.
