Our Hunzoria Activities

PKR 4,500

Hussaini Suspension Bridge

Cross one of the most photographed rope bridges in the world, over the Hunza river at Hussaini. Widely spaced planks, a long span, and a lot of wind.
  • Half day
  • Upper Hunza
PKR 6,000

Attabad Lake Boating

A boat out onto the turquoise lake formed by the 2010 landslide, with the drowned section of the old Karakoram Highway still visible below the surface.
  • Half day
  • Gojal, Hunza
PKR 12,000

Skiing at Naltar

Lift-served skiing at the Air Force resort above Naltar - the most accessible skiing in Pakistan. Beginners upward; hire and instruction arranged locally.
  • Full day
  • Naltar, Ghizer
PKR 7,500

Hopper Glacier Walk

Stand on a glacier twenty minutes from the road. The Hopper (Bualtar) glacier is black with moraine and genuinely enormous.
  • Full day
  • Nagar Valley
PKR 3,500

Blossom Season Walks

Guided walks through the apricot and cherry orchards in late March and April, when the whole valley floor turns white and pink. Seasonal.
  • Half day
  • Karimabad, Hunza
PKR 9,000

Trout Fishing at Phander

The Ghizer river and Phander Lake hold good brown trout. Permits, rods and a local guide who knows where the fish actually are.
  • Full day
  • Phander, Ghizer
