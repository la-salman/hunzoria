About Fairy Meadows and Nanga Parbat

Fairy Meadows sits above Raikot Bridge in Diamer district, and the approach is famous: a narrow, unguarded jeep track up from the Karakoram Highway, then a walk of two to three hours to the meadow.

The reward is Nanga Parbat - 8,126 metres, the ninth-highest mountain in the world - filling the sky from a pine-fringed alpine meadow. From there the trail continues to Beyal and the base camp viewpoint.

What to see
  • The Raikot Bridge to Tato jeep track
  • Fairy Meadows and its wooden huts
  • Beyal camp and the Nanga Parbat base camp viewpoint
  • Sunrise on the Raikot face
  • Pine forest and meadow walking
