K2 Base Camp and Concordia Trek

PKR 495,000/person
Duration 18 Days
Difficulty Difficult
Group Size 6-12 People
Max Altitude 5,150 m
About this trip

Concordia is where the Baltoro and Godwin-Austen glaciers meet, and it is ringed by four of the world's fourteen 8,000-metre peaks. Getting there means roughly two weeks of walking on glacier and moraine, camping every night, with no road access and no easy exit.

This is the most demanding trip we run. It requires previous multi-day trekking experience, genuine fitness, and comfort with basic conditions for an extended period. It is also, by common consent, one of the great treks on the planet.

Trip highlights
  • Concordia - K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I and II from one place
  • K2 base camp and the Gilkey Memorial
  • Trango Towers and Masherbrum from the Baltoro
  • Askole to Paiju along the Braldu gorge
  • Full expedition support with cook crew and porters
What's included
  • All camping equipment, tents and cook crew
  • Porters for group and personal loads
  • Certified high-altitude trekking guide
  • All meals on trek
  • Central Karakoram National Park permits and fees
  • Transport Skardu-Askole-Skardu
Not included
  • Personal trekking and cold-weather gear
  • Mandatory trekking insurance with helicopter evacuation cover
  • Hotel nights in Skardu beyond the itinerary
  • Tips for crew and porters
Itinerary
Day 1-2 – Skardu and preparation
Arrive Skardu, gear check, permits and briefing. One spare day built in for the Skardu flight, which is weather-dependent.
Day 3 – Skardu to Askole
Long, rough jeep drive to Askole, the last village on the route.
Day 4 – Askole to Jhola
First trekking day along the Braldu river. Around 7 hours.
Day 5 – Jhola to Paiju
Continue up the valley with the first views of the Trango group.
Day 6 – Rest day at Paiju
Acclimatisation day and porter preparation before stepping onto the glacier.
Day 7 – Paiju to Khoburtse
Onto the Baltoro glacier proper. Slow, rocky going.
Day 8 – Khoburtse to Urdukas
Camp on a grassy shelf above the glacier with Masherbrum opposite.
Day 9-10 – Urdukas to Goro II to Concordia
Two days up the glacier to Concordia at 4,600 m.
Day 11 – Concordia to K2 base camp
Day walk up the Godwin-Austen glacier to K2 base camp and the Gilkey Memorial, returning to Concordia.
Day 12 – Rest or Broad Peak base camp
Spare day for weather, or an optional walk to Broad Peak base camp.
Day 13-16 – Return down the Baltoro
Retrace the glacier and valley to Askole over four days.
Day 17 – Askole to Skardu
Jeep back to Skardu. Hot shower, real bed.
Day 18 – Depart Skardu
Fly or drive out.
