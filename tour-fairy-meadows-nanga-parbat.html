Fairy Meadows and Nanga Parbat Base Camp

PKR 125,000/person
Duration 6 Days
Difficulty Moderate
Group Size 4-10 People
Max Altitude 4,000 m
About this trip

Getting to Fairy Meadows is part of the story: a jeep track up from Raikot Bridge that is genuinely exposed, then an hour or two on foot or by mule to the meadow itself. What waits at the top is a grass shelf at 3,300 metres with Nanga Parbat filling the entire southern sky.

We stay two nights so there is a full day for the walk out to Beyal and the base camp viewpoint. The trail is not technical, but it is long and at altitude.

Trip highlights
  • The Raikot Bridge jeep track - one of the more memorable roads anywhere
  • Two nights in wooden huts at Fairy Meadows
  • Day hike to Beyal camp and the Nanga Parbat base camp viewpoint
  • Sunrise and sunset on the Raikot face
  • Pine forest and alpine meadow walking
What's included
  • Jeep transfer from Raikot Bridge
  • Two nights in Fairy Meadows huts, one night in Chilas
  • Guide and porter support
  • All meals at Fairy Meadows
  • Park fees
Not included
  • Transport to Raikot Bridge
  • Mule or porter hire for personal bags
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips
Itinerary
Day 1 – Islamabad or Gilgit to Chilas
Long driving day along the Karakoram Highway to Chilas. Overnight in a simple hotel.
Day 2 – Raikot Bridge to Fairy Meadows
Jeep up the Raikot track to Tato, then walk roughly 3 hours to Fairy Meadows. Afternoon at the meadow.
Day 3 – Beyal camp and base camp viewpoint
Full day hike to Beyal and on towards the Nanga Parbat base camp viewpoint. Around 7 hours return.
Day 4 – Fairy Meadows to Gilgit
Walk down to Tato, jeep to Raikot Bridge, then drive north to Gilgit.
Day 5 – Gilgit and Rakaposhi viewpoint
Gentler day - Gilgit bazaar, the Kargah Buddha carving, and the Rakaposhi viewpoint.
Day 6 – Depart Gilgit
Transfer to the airport or the road south.
