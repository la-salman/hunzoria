About Hunza Valley

Hunza sits at around 2,400 metres on the Karakoram Highway, with Rakaposhi on one side and Ultar Sar directly above Karimabad. The valley has been a trading corridor for centuries, and the two forts - Baltit and Altit - are the physical record of that.

It is also the easiest part of Gilgit-Baltistan to travel in. The road is paved, the guesthouses are good, and you can spend a week here without a single hard day of walking. Spring blossom in April and the apricot harvest in July are the two obvious times to come; autumn colour in late October is arguably better than both.

What to see
  • Baltit Fort and Altit Fort, Karimabad
  • Duikar / Eagle's Nest sunrise viewpoint
  • Attabad Lake
  • Passu Cones and the Hussaini suspension bridge
  • Khunjerab Pass and the China border
