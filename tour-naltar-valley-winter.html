Naltar Valley Winter and Ski Week

PKR 135,000/person
Duration 6 Days
Difficulty Easy-Moderate
Group Size 4-10 People
Max Altitude 3,000 m
About this trip

Naltar is a forested side valley an hour of rough jeep track above Gilgit, and in winter it is the best place in the country to ski. The Pakistan Air Force runs a small resort with a chairlift, and the surrounding slopes are wide, quiet and mostly empty.

Suitable for confident beginners upwards. Instruction and equipment hire can be arranged locally. In summer the same valley is about the lakes rather than the snow.

Trip highlights
  • Lift-served skiing at the Naltar ski resort
  • The Naltar coloured lakes - blue, green and white
  • Jeep track up from Nomal through the pine forest
  • Snowshoe walking for non-skiers
  • Wood-stove guesthouse evenings
What's included
  • Five nights guesthouse accommodation in Naltar
  • 4x4 transfers from Gilgit
  • All meals
  • Local guide
  • Ski resort access
Not included
  • Ski and boot hire
  • Instruction
  • Travel insurance with winter sports cover
  • Tips
Itinerary
Day 1 – Arrive Gilgit
Arrive and overnight in Gilgit.
Day 2 – Gilgit to Naltar
Jeep up the Naltar track through Nomal. Afternoon settling in and a short walk.
Day 3 – Ski day
Full day at the resort, or snowshoe walking in the forest for non-skiers.
Day 4 – Naltar lakes
Walk or ride up to the coloured lakes, frozen and quiet in winter.
Day 5 – Second ski day
Another day on the slopes, or a longer forest walk towards upper Naltar.
Day 6 – Return to Gilgit
Jeep back down and transfer for onward travel.
